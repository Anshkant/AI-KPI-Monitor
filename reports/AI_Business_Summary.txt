### ⚠️ Sales Performance Alert

**Executive Summary:**
Sales moderated compared with the previous period. The largest decline in transaction velocity was observed in Region South-West, while Product Category Furniture remained resilient and stable.

**Key Business Drivers:**
- **Territory Contribution**: Region East dominates top-line volume with 10.2% market share; Region South-West presents an addressable expansion gap.
- **Product Leadership**: Category Office Supplies generates highest unit throughput, while Furniture exhibits consistent repeat orders.
- **Channel Efficiency**: Offline sales channels deliver the highest conversion rate and lowest customer acquisition friction.

**Operational Risk Assessment:**
Operational return rate stands at 49.9%. 995 transaction anomalies detected requiring pricing audit

**Recommended Strategic Actions:**
1. Investigate Region South-West's order volume, logistics SLAs, and regional conversion rates.
2. Scale inventory and promotional budgets for top-selling Category Office Supplies ahead of high-demand cycles.
3. Audit discount threshold compliance on outlier transactions to safeguard the 27.0% operating profit margin.
